# OffTrack Journal — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
**App:** OffTrack Journal (Bundle ID `com.dayback.offplan`)
**Developer:** [Developer / Company Name]

This Privacy Policy explains what information OffTrack Journal (the "App") handles and how. It is written for the App Store "Privacy Policy URL" requirement and for users who want to understand our data practices.

---

## 1. Summary

OffTrack Journal is designed to work **entirely offline**. The App does **not** collect, upload, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you create stays on your own device. We operate no server and run no analytics.

## 2. Data We Collect

**We collect no data.**

- No account, no sign-up, no login.
- No usage analytics, no crash reporting that leaves the device, no advertising identifiers (no IDFA).
- No third-party SDKs of any kind.

Because the App never sends anything to a server, we never receive your entries, photos, or behavior.

## 3. Data Storage

All content you enter — text, optional photos (stored compressed on-device), dates, categories, and evaluation status — is saved in a **local database (SwiftData)** on your device, protected by iOS Data Protection.

- Uninstalling the App deletes this data permanently; we cannot recover it.
- There is no cloud sync and no backup to our infrastructure. (Standard iCloud device backups, if you enable them in iOS, are encrypted and managed by Apple — not by us.)

## 4. Network Usage

The App makes **no network requests** in normal operation. It contains no networking code paths that contact external servers, no third-party SDKs, and no advertising or analytics frameworks.

> A remote-notification (push) capability is present in the build for possible future server-side reminders. In the current version it is **not active** and **no data is sent to any server**. When enabled later, any push payload will contain no personal content — only a generic local reminder trigger.

## 5. Biometric Data (Face ID / Touch ID)

Face ID / Touch ID is used **only** to unlock the App locally. The operating system returns a simple success/failure result; we cannot access, view, or store your biometric information, and it never leaves your device.

## 6. Notifications

Revisit reminders are **local notifications** scheduled by the system on your device — they pass through no server. Notification permission is requested on first launch and can be changed at any time in iOS Settings → OffTrack Journal → Notifications. Denying it affects only reminders and no other feature.

## 7. Photo Library

"Save Image" (used by the Year Review export) requests **photo-library add-only** permission. It is used solely to write images you choose to save. The App does **not** read your photo library.

## 8. In-App Purchases

All payments are processed by the Apple App Store. We never receive or store your payment information. Subscription and purchase management (renewal, cancellation, refund) is handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account settings.

- Yearly: auto-renewable subscription, **$0.99/year**.
- Lifetime: one-time purchase, **$7.99**.

## 9. Children

The App is rated **4+** and is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from any user — including children — because the App collects no data at all.

## 10. Your Rights / Data Deletion

Because no data leaves your device, there is no account to delete and no server-side data to remove.

- To delete all content: delete the App, or remove individual entries within the App (long-press an entry → delete). Deletion is immediate and local.
- We have no records to export or erase on your behalf.

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date above.
