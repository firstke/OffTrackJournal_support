# OffTrack Journal — Support

Welcome to **OffTrack Journal**. Most apps track what you planned. This one tracks what you didn't — and asks you again 7 days later whether it was worth it.

This page covers getting started, subscriptions, and troubleshooting. For our data practices, see the [Privacy Policy](Privacy-Policy.md).

---

## Getting Started

1. **Record** — Tap "+", write one sentence (add an optional photo). Takes about 10 seconds.
2. **Revisit** — A local reminder fires on **day 7 at 10:00 AM** asking you to reconsider the moment.
3. **Judge** — Choose **Worth it / Not worth it / Unsure**. No verdict from today's mood.
4. **Year Review** — See where your unplanned moments happened and how they turned out (paid feature).

The core loop — recording and the 7-day revisit — is **free forever**.

## Subscription & Purchases

| Plan | Type | Price | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Yearly** | Auto-renewable subscription | **$0.99 / year** | Unlimited entries, Year Review, long-image / PDF export, Face ID lock |
| **Lifetime** | One-time purchase | **$7.99** | All features, permanently |

- **Free tier:** limited to **100 entries**. Exceeding the limit routes you to the paywall.

### Manage or cancel a subscription
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us:

- iOS: **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → OffTrack Journal**.
- The yearly plan auto-renews within 24 hours before expiry unless you turn it off beforehand.
- You can cancel anytime; access continues until the end of the current period.

### Restore purchases
On a new device, tap **Restore Purchases** at the bottom of the paywall to recover your entitlements.

## Face ID Lock

- Off by default. Enable at **Settings → Privacy → Face ID Lock**.
- On devices without Face ID / Touch ID, the App unlocks automatically and never blocks the UI.

---

## Notifications (Local Reminders)

- Permission is requested on first launch.
- If you denied it, re-enable at **iOS Settings → OffTrack Journal → Notifications**.
- Reminders are scheduled **locally** on your device — no internet connection is required.

---

## Save Image (Year Review Export)

"Save Image" uses **photo-library add-only** permission to write the exported image. If denied, the App shows an inline message; grant access in **iOS Settings → OffTrack Journal → Photos**.

## Troubleshooting (FAQ)

**Q: I didn't get my 7-day reminder.**
A: Check notification permission (iOS Settings → OffTrack Journal → Notifications). Reminders are local, so the device must be powered on at the scheduled time. Also confirm the entry was saved — only saved entries are scheduled.

**Q: Where is my data stored?**
A: Entirely on your device (local SwiftData). We operate no server and cannot see or recover your data. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

**Q: Face ID doesn't prompt / won't unlock.**
A: Ensure Face ID Lock is enabled in **Settings → Privacy → Face ID Lock**, and that the device supports Face ID / Touch ID. On unsupported devices the App unlocks automatically.

**Q: How do I delete an entry?**
A: Long-press the entry and choose delete. It is removed immediately and locally.

**Q: I was charged but features are still locked.**
A: Tap **Restore Purchases** on the paywall. If it still doesn't unlock, send us your Apple receipt from **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions / Purchase History** and we'll help.

**Q: Can I use the App on iPad?**
A: Yes — the App runs on iPhone and is compatible with iPad. Some layouts are iPhone-optimized.
